# 🧟 Click-To-Kill-The-Zombies

👉 **[Play the game here](https://shubhneet-singh.github.io/Click-To-Kill-The-Zombies/)**

A fast-paced browser survival shooter. Zombies close in from every side — tap or click to blast them before they reach you, earn coins, and use them to unlock stronger guns and shields.

## Features
- 🧟 10,000 levels with increasing difficulty
- 🔫 7 unlockable weapons and 4 shields
- 👹 Boss battles at key level milestones
- ⏱️ 15-Second Challenge mode for quick bonus coins
- 💾 Auto-saves your progress, coins, and gear
- 📱 Works on both desktop and mobile — no install needed

## Built With
- HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript
- No frameworks, no build steps — just one self-contained file

## How to Play
Click or tap the zombies before they reach you. Earn coins for each kill, then open the shop to buy better weapons and shields. Survive boss levels, or jump into the 15-second challenge for a quick coin boost.
